Additional in-progress projects are supported by and contributing to the Male Sperm Whale Global Perspective Project, under the supervision of Christine Clarke and Hal Whitehead.

The following two projects are using drone data provided by Ana Eguiguren and David Gaspard (Whitehead Lab members) and the Marine Megafauna Research Group, University of Otago, Dunedin, New Zealand.

- An assessment of prevalence of skin sloughing in the Galápagos, New Zealand, and the Northeast Atlantic, by Charlotte Powell (Dalhousie University, BSc Graduate)
- An assessment of body condition of sperm whales in the Galápagos, New Zealand, and the Northeast Atlantic, by Rhianna Baker (Dalhousie University, Honours Student)
